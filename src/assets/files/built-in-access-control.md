## Built-In Access Control. Not Bolted On.

Most gym software manages your members. Kinect manages your members and your door —
from the same system, in real time. No third-party integrations. No sync delays. No extra
monthly fees.

### 1. Why "Native" Access Control Matters

Most access control systems are afterthoughts — separate software, separate hardware,
separate billing, duct-taped together with an API. When something breaks or falls out of sync,
you're stuck calling two different support teams.

Kinect is different. Access control is written directly into our core software. Your door and your
member database are the same system. That's what native means — and it changes
everything.

### 2. Security That Requires Physical Presence

Most mobile key apps will let a member unlock your door from the parking lot, their car, or their
couch. That's not access control — that's a liability.

Kinect's ReliaGate controller uses your facility Wi-Fi as a secure geofence. The unlock button
only appears when a member is physically on-site and connected to your network. You can run
unstaffed hours with confidence, knowing only people who are actually there can get in.

### 3. Your Members' Phones Are Their Keys

Plastic fobs cost $3–5 each, get lost constantly, and get shared. A member can hand a fob to a
friend and you'd never know.

With Kinect, the member's smartphone is their key — and it's device-bound. That means they
can't text their access to someone else. If they try to log in on a second device, their first one is
locked out automatically. No fob budget. No buddy passes. No exceptions.

### 4. Your Door Knows What Your Billing Knows

Because access and billing live in the same system, they stay in perfect sync — automatically.

- Missed payment? Access is revoked in milliseconds — not the next morning, not after a manual review.  
- New member signs up? They can walk through your door 30 seconds later.  
- Restricted plan? If their membership doesn't include 24/7 access, the unlock button simply doesn't appear.  

No manual overrides. No one slipping through on an expired membership. It just works.

### 5. One System. Not Three.

Most gym owners are juggling separate software for member management, payment
processing, and access control. That's three logins, three monthly bills, and three support teams
to call when something goes wrong.

Kinect replaces all of it. One dashboard. One bill. One place to go when you need help.

### 6. Is Kinect Right for Your Facility?

Kinect is purpose-built for a specific kind of gym owner. You're likely a great fit if:

- You have a stable on-site Wi-Fi connection (required for our presence-confirmation system)  
- You're ready to go fully smartphone-based and retire physical fobs entirely  
- You want to run lean — fewer front desk hours, lower overhead, tighter security
