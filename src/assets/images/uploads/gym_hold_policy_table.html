Gym Hold & Pause Policies

Industry Standards at a Glance

Category What Most Gyms Do
Notice Required7 to 10 days before the next billing cycle
How to RequestWritten request, email, or through a member app
Retroactive HoldsAlmost never allowed
Maximum Hold Length90 days per year, some cap at 60
Minimum Hold Length2 weeks to 1 month
Hold FeeAnywhere from $2.50 a week to $25 a month
Medical HoldsFee usually waived with a doctor's note
Access During HoldRevoked, key fob or app disabled
Early ReturnMember contacts gym to reactivate
Contract ExtensionHold time gets added to the end of your contract term
Cancellation While on HoldStandard cancellation notice still applies

Hometown Hero Tip: Waive the hold fee for medical needs. Flexibility builds loyalty no big-box gym can touch.
