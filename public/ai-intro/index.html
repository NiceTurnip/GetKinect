After years of running gyms and experimenting with keypad codes and key fobs, the team built a mobile‑first access system integrated directly into gym management software. Early adopters validated the model during 2020–2022, leading to a commercial launch focused on Canadian independent gyms and studios. Kinect’s differentiator is native access control inside the management platform. Members unlock the facility with their own phone only when on the gym’s Wi‑Fi, enforcing presence at the door and eliminating the cost and friction of key fobs. Owners and staff can unlock remotely from the dashboard, while the platform handles signups, waivers, billing, scheduling, and reporting. ##Core Service Offerings ###Primary Platform Capabilities * Smartphone Door Access (Wi‑Fi, not Bluetooth): Member device must be on the facility’s Wi‑Fi; prevents off‑site unlocks and credential sharing. * Access Schedules & Rules: Time‑based access by plan or temporary pass; real‑time logs and attendance. * Remote Unlock for Staff/Owners: One‑off entry from the dashboard; event is logged. * Membership & Plan Management: Unlimited members, plan rules, access windows. * Payments & Billing (Stripe and others): Online signups, automatic recurring billing, renewals and pausing. * Waivers & Compliance: Online signature flow; staff reset links hardened. * Classes/Events & Attendance: Scheduling, credits/punches, roster visibility. * Reporting & Analytics: Access and payment views in‑product; export options. ## Support & Services * Onboarding (1‑on‑1): Typical setup ~1 week once electric strike is present. * 24/7 Emergency Support: Incident response and troubleshooting guidance. * CSR Playbooks & KB: Lean how‑to templates, troubleshooting references, and training. Operating Principles & Security Model * Presence‑based access: Member must be on facility Wi‑Fi. If not, the app shows: “you are not on the facility’s Wi‑Fi.” * Device‑specific login: Member credentials are bound to a single device; sharing locks out the original device. Easy device replacement flow. * No Bluetooth, no kiosks, no fobs: Minimal hardware footprint; requires existing electric strike and the Kinect ReliaGate controller. * Staff password resets: Reset links are valid for 5 minutes due to elevated privileges. * Plan‑to‑Access linkage: If a plan has no access schedule attached, the member will not see the lock/unlock button in the app. ## Access Workflows Remote Unlock (one‑off) Path: Dashboard → Facilities → View → Remote Unlock (confirm). Effect: Immediate entry; event logged. Temporary Access Pass (short‑term; not a plan) Path: Temporary Passes → Create Temporary Pass. * Add recipient email; set start/end window (default ~1 hour). * Recipient verifies by email, accepts waiver, and must be on gym Wi‑Fi during the window. * After end time, pass auto‑expires; attendance visible under Temporary Passes.
